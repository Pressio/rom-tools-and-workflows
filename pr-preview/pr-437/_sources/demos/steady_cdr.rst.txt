Steady convection-diffusion-reaction model
==========================================

**Method.** The steady CDR equation combines diffusion, advection, and reaction in a two-dimensional domain.

**Example.** A small NumPy/SciPy full-order model and QoI wrapper provide a reproducible test problem for UQ and inverse workflows.

For governing equations, numerical setup, and complete model source, see
:doc:`steady_cdr_details`.
