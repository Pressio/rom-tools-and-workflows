H2-air flame model
==================

**Method.** A two-dimensional reacting-flow model evolves hydrogen, oxygen, water, and temperature fields.

**Example.** The pure-Python flame solver and QoI wrapper provide a nonlinear benchmark for hyper-reduction and multifidelity inverse workflows.

For governing equations, numerical setup, and complete model source, see
:doc:`h2_air_flame_details`.
