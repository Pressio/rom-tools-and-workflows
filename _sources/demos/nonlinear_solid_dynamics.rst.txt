Solid dynamics model
====================

**Method.** A two-dimensional finite-element solid-dynamics model simulates transient structural response.

**Example.** The NumPy/SciPy model and QoI wrapper support ROM, hyper-reduction, and inverse-workflow examples; the gallery shows cantilever and wave responses.

See :doc:`nonlinear_solid_dynamics_gallery` for generated results.

For governing equations, numerical setup, and complete model source, see
:doc:`nonlinear_solid_dynamics_details`.
